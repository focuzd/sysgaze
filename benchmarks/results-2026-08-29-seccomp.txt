Interpretation:
  Seccomp has little room to help when nearly every call is selected, as in the
  syscall-heavy workload. It is most effective when selected calls are sparse.
  The filter is inherited by forked processes and threads, and its advantage
  remains measurable at 16 workers. No single-run performance claim is used.
